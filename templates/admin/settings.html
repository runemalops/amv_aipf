SMTP / Email Settings

Configure SMTP settings to enable email notifications when visitors submit contact form messages.

For Gmail, use an App Password instead of your regular password.
The email address used as the sender.
Where contact form messages will be sent.
SMTP Configuration Examples

  • SMTP Server: smtp.gmail.com
  • Port: 587
  • Username: your-email@gmail.com
  • Password: 16-character App Password (enable 2FA first)
  • Sender Email: your-email@gmail.com
  • TLS: Enabled

  • SMTP Server: smtp-mail.outlook.com
  • Port: 587
  • Username: your-email@outlook.com
  • Password: Your account password
  • Sender Email: your-email@outlook.com
  • TLS: Enabled

  • Use the SMTP credentials provided by your email service
  • Common ports: 587 (TLS) or 465 (SSL)
  • Check your service's documentation for server address
Tips
  • For Gmail, you need to generate an App Password after enabling 2-Factor Authentication.
  • The Contact Recipient is where you'll receive messages from the contact form.
  • If SMTP is not configured, messages will still be saved to the database.
  • Test your configuration by submitting a contact form after saving.
